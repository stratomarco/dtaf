# Residual risk register

| Risk ID | Threat / check / control IDs | Exposure and uncertainty | Evidence / limitations | Treatment and compensating controls | Implementation owner / due date | Acceptance authority | Decision / rationale / date | Expiry / review trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-<id> | <IDs> | <remaining risk> | <E-IDs or unknown> | <mitigate, avoid, transfer, propose acceptance> | <name / date> | <authorized role or unresolved> | <Pending, Accepted, Rejected, Closed; reason and evidence> | <date / change> |

Capture risks from completed assessments and effective controls as well as gaps. Assignment is not acceptance. An unknown authority or unsigned proposal remains Pending. Closure requires evidence and an authorized decision, including why exposure no longer applies.
